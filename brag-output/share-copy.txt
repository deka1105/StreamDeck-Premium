Introducing DeskAssist: your phone is the button. Tap a tile and your Mac
launches an app, fires a shortcut, or types saved text — nine tiles, one tap each.

Scan once to pair, then every press is sealed end-to-end with X25519 and
AES-256-GCM. No cloud, no account, nothing leaves your network.

Built with Expo and React Native, an Electron host, and RevenueCat.
