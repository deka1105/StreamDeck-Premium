What DeskAssist does

DeskAssist lets your phone trigger actions on a computer you control: launching applications, opening links, sending keyboard shortcuts, and typing saved text. It works only after you pair the two devices by scanning a code shown on that computer.

Understand what you are enabling. A paired phone can launch applications and send keystrokes to your computer. Treat pairing like handing someone your keyboard: pair only devices you own or trust, on networks you trust, and unpair a phone you no longer control. The host application's shell-command capability is disabled by default and should only be enabled if you understand that it permits arbitrary commands to run on that computer.
