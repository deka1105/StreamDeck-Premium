# Demo video — shooting script

**Rig:** phone A films, phone B runs the app, Mac monitor in frame.
**Target:** ~90 seconds. **No music.** Captions burned in.

---

## Do this before you shoot

1. **Grant Pro via RevenueCat**, or you cannot film Auto mode or multiple pages.
   Dashboard → **Customers** → your device's App User ID (most recent) → **Grant
   Entitlement** → `deskassist_pro`. This works without StoreKit, so it is
   unaffected by the agreement/IAP situation. If you skip it, use the
   **free-tier variant** of takes 4 and 5 below.
2. **Grant the host Accessibility.** System Settings → Privacy & Security →
   Accessibility → DeskAssist → remove with **−**, add with **+**, switch on.
   Without this, snippets and Spotlight silently do nothing — takes 1 and 3 die.
3. **Pair phone B to the Mac for real.** Demo mode cannot show the Mac reacting,
   which is the whole video.
4. **Open a TextEdit window on the Mac** with the cursor in it, for take 3.
5. **Clean the Mac:** clear the desktop, tidy the Dock, close anything with your
   name or private data in it, Do Not Disturb on.
6. **Phone B:** max brightness, auto-brightness off, Do Not Disturb on.
7. **Phone A (camera):** Do Not Disturb, landscape, propped on something solid —
   you need both hands free. Tap and **hold to lock focus and exposure**, or it
   will hunt every time a screen changes brightness.

**Framing:** Mac monitor fills the right two-thirds, phone B stands in the lower
left, both screens readable. Shoot slightly off-perpendicular to the Mac panel
to kill reflections. Dim the room; no window or lamp behind the screens.

**Audio:** film silent. A camera a metre away records bad sound. Record the
voice-over separately afterwards on phone A's voice memo app and lay it under —
or skip voice entirely and run captions only.

Shoot each take separately so a fluffed beat costs one clip, not the whole video.

---

## Take 1 — Proof, with no preamble (0:00–0:09)

**Action.** Deck on phone B, Mac desktop behind. Tap **Safari** → Safari opens on
the Mac. Two-beat pause. Tap **Spotlight** → Spotlight opens on the Mac.

**Voice-over.** *(none — let it play)*

**Caption,** appearing at ~0:04:
> Tap a tile. Your Mac does it.

No logo, no title card. The first five seconds decide whether anyone watches the
rest, and "I touch this, that happens" needs no explanation.

---

## Take 2 — What it is (0:09–0:18)

**Action.** Hold steady on the full nine-tile deck. No interaction.

**Voice-over.**
> My phone is a Stream Deck for my Mac. Nine tiles, one tap each — apps, links,
> keyboard shortcuts.

**Caption.**
> A Stream Deck — on the phone you already own.

One plain sentence. Nothing here should contain the word "encryption" yet.

---

## Take 3 — The money shot (0:18–0:36)

**Action.** TextEdit focused on the Mac, cursor blinking. On phone B: tap
**Snippets** → tap **"Thanks for the quick turnaround."** → the sentence types
itself into the Mac window. **Hold three full seconds on the typed line.**

**Voice-over.**
> This is text I retype all the time. One tap, and it types itself — straight
> into whatever window I'm working in.

**Caption.**
> Saved text, typed for you.

The most surprising thing the app does. Do not rush the hold.

---

## Take 4 — Auto mode (0:36–0:50)

**Action.** Tap **🪄 Auto** on phone B. Then on the Mac, switch to VS Code — the
deck changes by itself. Switch to Finder — it changes again. Keep hands off the
phone while it happens.

**Voice-over.**
> Turn on Auto, and the deck follows whatever app I'm in. It switches itself.

**Caption.**
> Auto mode: the deck follows your focused app.

> **Free-tier variant** (no Pro granted): instead, open profile settings, show
> the trigger-app list and the "Use current app" button, then say:
> *"Auto mode switches the deck to match the app you're in — which is the point
> at which one deck stops being enough."* Then cut straight to take 7. It turns
> the limitation into the upgrade argument, honestly.

---

## Take 5 — It's a real app (0:50–1:00)

**Action.** Tap **Edit**. Drag a tile to a new position. Tap the **✎** badge on a
tile and change its emoji and colour. Add a **page** — the dots appear below the
grid.

**Voice-over.**
> Every tile is editable on the phone. Drag to reorder, any emoji or your own
> image, as many pages as you want.

**Caption.**
> Fully editable. No config files.

> **Free-tier variant:** drop "as many pages as you want" and the page-add
> action; just do the drag and the icon change.

---

## Take 6 — It knows your machine (1:00–1:08)

**Action.** Tap the display icon in the header → the **Apps** list of what's
running on the Mac. Tap one → it comes to the front on the Mac. Point out a
green running dot on a deck tile.

**Voice-over.**
> It knows what's actually running. Tap any app to bring it to the front.

**Caption.**
> Live: what's open, and what's in front.

Say "running" out loud rather than relying on the dot being green.

---

## Take 7 — Monetization (1:08–1:18)

**Action.** Trigger the paywall by hitting a limit, not by tapping the Pro pill —
try to add a **fourth snippet**. The reason-aware message appears. Show the three
tiers.

**Voice-over.**
> Free gives you a working deck. Pro removes the limits — more profiles, more
> pages, unlimited snippets.

**Caption.**
> Free works. Pro removes the limits.

Hitting a limit is better than the Pro pill because it shows *why* someone pays.

> If the paywall still shows "no pricing available", film the limit message
> itself and stop there. Do not film the error.

---

## Take 8 — Trust, and close (1:18–1:30)

**Action.** Cut to the Mac showing the pairing QR, then back to the deck on the
phone. Hold on the deck to end.

**Voice-over.**
> You pair it by scanning a QR code once. After that every tap is end-to-end
> encrypted, straight from my phone to my Mac. No account, no cloud — nothing
> leaves my network.

**Caption,** three short lines:
> Scan once to pair
> End-to-end encrypted
> No account. No cloud.

Final frame: the deck, with **DeskAssist** over it. End on the name; don't trail
off.

## Caption rules

- At least ~40px at 1080p. Thin light type over a bright screen vanishes.
- On a dimmed strip, not straight over UI.
- Hold each line long enough to read twice — roughly 0.3s per word, never under
  ~1.5s.
- Most people watch muted, so captions are not optional.

## Full voice-over, as one block

For recording in a single pass, then cutting to the takes:

> My phone is a Stream Deck for my Mac. Nine tiles, one tap each — apps, links,
> keyboard shortcuts.
>
> This is text I retype all the time. One tap, and it types itself — straight

> Turn on Auto, and the deck follows whatever app I'm in. It switches itself.
>
> Every tile is editable on the phone. Drag to reorder, any emoji or your own
> image, as many pages as you want.
>
> It knows what's actually running. Tap any app to bring it to the front.
>
> Free gives you a working deck. Pro removes the limits — more profiles, more
> pages, unlimited snippets.
>
> You pair it by scanning a QR code once. After that every tap is end-to-end

~140 words. Read unhurried, that is about 60 seconds of speech across a
90-second cut, which leaves room to breathe.
