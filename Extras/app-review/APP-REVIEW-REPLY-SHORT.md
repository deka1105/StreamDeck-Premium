Thank you for the review. Answers below, in order.

**1. Screen recording** — attached, captured on a physical iPhone running the
current iOS release (build 6), beginning at app launch.

Please note: **you do not need a Mac, and you do not need an account, to review
this app.** DeskAssist controls a computer, so a reviewer will not have the
companion Mac app. On the first screen, tap **"Explore the demo instead"** — the
whole app then runs against a simulated Mac, with a standing banner saying so.
No pairing, no network access and no account.

- **Accounts:** none. No sign-up, sign-in or profile exists, so there is no
  account-deletion flow to show.
- **User-generated content:** not shared. Snippets, tile labels and tile images
  stay on the device and are never uploaded or made visible to any other user,
  so there is no feed, messaging, reporting or blocking surface.
- **Paid content:** the paywall and each free-tier limit that opens it are shown
  in the recording.

**2. Purpose and audience** — DeskAssist turns an iPhone into a control surface
for your computer: one tap launches an app, opens a URL, sends a keyboard
shortcut, or types a saved block of text. It replaces $100–250 hardware control
decks with the phone you already own. It is for people who repeat the same
computer actions all day — developers, streamers, video editors, designers, and
support staff who retype the same replies.

**3. Setup and access** — no login credentials and no sample files are required;
there is no account. Tap "Explore the demo instead" and the deck, Snippets,
Apps, Auto mode and Edit mode are all usable immediately. To reach the in-app
purchase, tap the **Pro** pill in the header or hit any free-tier limit: a
second profile, a second page, a fourth snippet, or an image tile face. Free
allows 1 profile, 1 page, 3 snippets and emoji/text tile faces.

**4. External services** — Apple In-App Purchase (the only payment path in the
app), RevenueCat (reads the entitlement Apple returns), and Expo / React Native
(framework). No analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no advertising, no
authentication provider, no AI service, and no backend of ours. All
cryptography runs locally on the device via the open-source `@noble` libraries
(X25519, HKDF, AES-256-GCM).

**5. Regional differences** — none. The app behaves identically in every
region: no geo-gating, no region-specific content or features, no
region-restricted services. The interface is English only.

**6. Regulated industry / third-party material** — neither applies. The app
handles no health, financial, medical or government data and provides no
regulated service. Tile labels such as "Safari" are user-editable text naming an
app on the user's own computer, and the default icons are standard Unicode
emoji, not third-party logos or artwork.

No demo account is required. We would be grateful for any expedited
consideration you can give this submission, as we are working to a fixed launch
date, and we are happy to provide anything further you need.
