IMPORTANT: you do not need a Mac, and you do not need an account, to review
this app in full. There are also no in-app purchases.

WHAT CHANGED IN THIS BUILD
The previous rejection (13 Nov 2026) cited guideline 2.1(b) for an error on the
In-App Purchase page and 3.1.2(c) for the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links.

Both are resolved by removal rather than repair: the paywall, the three products
and the payment SDK have been taken out of the app entirely. DeskAssist is now
free, with every feature available to everyone - unlimited profiles, pages and
snippets, custom image tiles, and all five action types. There is no purchase
screen left to show an error, and no auto-renewing subscription, so 3.1.2(c) no
longer applies.

The three in-app purchase products remain attached to the previous submission
and could not be detached through the API. They are not referenced by the
binary, which contains no payment SDK.

The Privacy Policy, Support and Marketing URLs were also moved to a host with no
payment dependency, after the previous host began returning HTTP 402:
  https://deka1105.github.io/StreamDeck-Premium/privacy.html

HOW TO REVIEW WITHOUT A MAC
On the first screen, tap "Explore the demo instead". The entire app then runs
against a simulated Mac:
  - the deck of tiles renders and responds to taps
  - the running-apps screen populates with a simulated app list
  - Auto mode reacts to a rotating "focused app"
  - the snippet library sends text and reports the result
A standing banner states that it is a simulation and that nothing is actually
sent. No pairing, no network access and no account are required.

NO ACCOUNTS
The app has no sign-up, no sign-in and no user accounts of any kind, so there is
no account-deletion flow. No demo credentials are needed because there is
nothing to log in to.

NO USER-GENERATED CONTENT
Snippets, tile labels and tile images are stored only on the device. Nothing is
uploaded, shared or visible to any other user, so there is no feed, no
messaging, and therefore no content reporting or blocking mechanism.

WITH A MAC (entirely optional, not needed for review)
The companion Mac host app shows a QR code. Scanning it performs an X25519 key
exchange; every command afterwards is AES-256-GCM encrypted with a per-device
key and sent directly from the phone to the Mac over the local network. There is
no account and no server of ours in the path. The Mac host is distributed
separately as a free open-source download and is not required to evaluate the
iPhone app.

PLATFORM NOTES
Launching applications and opening URLs work on macOS and Windows. Sending
keyboard shortcuts and detecting the focused application are macOS-only and
require macOS Accessibility permission, which the host app requests on the
computer. None of this affects the demo mode used for review.

REGIONS
The app behaves identically in every region. There is no geo-gating, no
region-specific content and no regional feature differences. All text is
English only.
