IMPORTANT: you do not need a Mac, and you do not need an account, to review
this app in full.

DeskAssist turns an iPhone into a control surface for a computer. Because a
reviewer will not have the companion Mac app running, the app ships a complete
simulated mode so every feature can be exercised on the device alone.

HOW TO REVIEW WITHOUT A MAC
On the first screen, tap "Explore the demo instead". The entire app then runs
against a simulated Mac:
  - the 3x3 deck of tiles renders and responds to taps
  - the running-apps screen populates with a simulated app list
  - Auto mode reacts to a rotating "focused app"
  - the snippet library sends text and reports the result
A standing banner states that it is a simulation and that nothing is actually
sent. No pairing, no network access, and no account are required.

NO ACCOUNTS
The app has no sign-up, no sign-in, and no user accounts of any kind, so there
is no account-deletion flow. Nothing is required to reach full functionality.
No demo credentials are needed because there is nothing to log in to.

IN-APP PURCHASE (DeskAssist Pro)
Open the paywall either by tapping the "Pro" pill in the header, or by reaching
any free-tier limit:
  - adding a second profile          (free tier allows 1)
  - adding a second page             (free tier allows 1)
  - adding a fourth snippet          (free tier allows 3)
  - choosing an image as a tile face (free tier allows emoji and text only)
Pro removes all four limits. Purchases are processed by Apple In-App Purchase;
entitlements are read through RevenueCat. There is no non-Apple payment path
anywhere in the app.

NO USER-GENERATED CONTENT
Snippets, tile labels and tile images are stored only on the device. Nothing is
uploaded, shared, or visible to any other user, so there is no feed, no
messaging, and therefore no content reporting or blocking mechanism.

WITH A MAC (entirely optional, not needed for review)
The companion Mac host app shows a QR code. Scanning it performs an X25519 key
exchange; every command afterwards is AES-256-GCM encrypted with a per-device
key and sent directly from the phone to the Mac over the local network. There
is no account and no server of ours in the path. The Mac host is distributed
separately as a free open-source download and is not required to evaluate the
iPhone app.

PLATFORM NOTES
Launching applications and opening URLs work on macOS and Windows. Sending
keyboard shortcuts and detecting the focused application are macOS-only and
require macOS Accessibility permission, which the host app requests on the
computer. None of this affects the demo mode used for review.

REGIONS
The app behaves identically in every region. There is no geo-gating, no
region-specific content, and no regional feature differences. All text is
English only.
