IMPORTANT: you do not need a Mac to review this app.

DeskAssist is a companion app for a Mac. To review it without one, tap "Explore
the demo instead" on the first screen. The whole app then runs against a
simulated Mac: the deck renders and responds to taps, the running-apps list
populates, Auto mode reacts to a rotating focused app, and the snippet library
sends text. A standing banner makes clear it is a simulation, and no pairing or
network access is needed.

IN-APP PURCHASE
Tap the "Pro" pill in the header to open the paywall, or reach any free-tier
limit: adding a second profile, a second page, a fourth snippet, or choosing an
image tile face.

WITH A MAC (optional)
The free DeskAssist host app displays a QR code. Scanning it performs an X25519
key exchange; every command afterwards is AES-256-GCM encrypted and sent
directly from the phone to the Mac over the local network. There is no account,
and no server of ours is in the path. Host download: https://deskassist.vercel.app

PLATFORM NOTES
Launching applications and opening URLs work on macOS and Windows. Sending
keyboard shortcuts and detecting the focused application are macOS-only and
require macOS Accessibility permission, which the host application requests on
the computer.

No demo account is required.
